Homework: Illustrated Enviorment

For this assignment, I used poll data and turned it into a visual format that illustrates the media habits of students at hunter college. I was curious about how much art and media students consumed regularly so I gathered data on how many books students were reading, movies they were watching, and times they visited theaters each month. I chose to display this data with a Pie chart, bar chart, and a line graph. For the colors I chose to use different shades of red and orange because it is the end of fall and almost thanksgiving time.
