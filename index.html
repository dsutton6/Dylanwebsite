Dylans Portfolio

Dylan Sutton is a Brooklyn based artist working through the mediums of Filmmaking, Photography, and Narrative fiction Screenwriting. I view my camera as a tool to eternalize and archive my world and the people who inhabit it by capturing their essence with care and authenticity. In my work I strive to immerse the viewer in a sense of nostalgia and sentimentality through a use of intimate Documentary and quiet style photography. Although I capture moments spontaneously in the moment, I hope for my photography and films to feel like the recalling of a forgotten memory or the small overlooked moments in between the big ones. I am inspired greatly by artists such as Todd Hido, Kelly Reichardt, and Gus Van Sandt.

I am a current student at Hunter college working on a degree in Media Studies and Documentary production. I hope to work within the film industry post grad and eventually direct my own films.
