The massive stone lions guard the steps as you approach the library. The air is cool and smells faintly of old paper and city smog. You step through the towering doors, and the noise of 5th Avenue vanishes instantly.

You feel a sense of overwhelming quiet. You know you need to find a specific book, but the place is a labyrinth. You see two main paths ahead: a brightly lit, wide marble stairway, and a small, shadowed sign pointing toward a side archive hallway.

What do you do?

1. Ascend the grand, marble staircase to the Rose Reading Room. 2. Follow the small, shadowed sign to the archives hallway.
