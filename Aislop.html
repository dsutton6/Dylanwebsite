AI Slop

In her article “With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster,” Nesrine Malik details our current media landscape in relation to the new emerging influx of content created from artificial intelligence and its consequences that we are seeing in real time. Throughout the article she talks about how AI content is being used for right-wing misinformation and fantasy scenarios. She specifically states this artificial content leads to the desensitization and overwhelming of visual palates. “The overall effect of being exposed to AI images all the time, from the nonsensical to the soothing to the ideological, is that everything begins to land in a different way.” I think this really encapsulates my personal feelings and fears toward the emergence and surplus of this kind of content. I believe that people's perception of reality is being warped and desensitized to a level we have never seen before. The mass amounts of content that I am subjected to on a daily basis leave me feeling physically drained at times. We are being fed a constant stream of real-world violence, as well as fake content with the intention of soothing while real atrocities are being committed around the world in places like Gaza and to immigrants in our own communities. I think the line between reality and fake content is beginning to blur, and it's becoming hard for some to differentiate between the two.

I think the rise of artificial intelligence and increase of online activity are depleting us of empathy and the need for human connection, furthering the division of our world. In the article she also states, “I am in a constant struggle with an otherwise online-savvy elderly relative who receives and believes a deluge of content created by artificial intelligence on WhatsApp about Sudan’s war.” I think artificial intelligence-made content is a major issue for older generations who did not grow up with this technology and are even more susceptible to this misinformation. I also have family members who fall into these false rabbit holes of political AI content, and this is another thing that causes division within our world and is also impacting familial relationships. Another point she poses in the article is the use of AI for trad wife and white supremacy content. She says, “X timelines are awash with a sort of clothed nonsexual pornography, as AI images of women described as comely, fertile, and submissive glimmer on the screen. White supremacy, autocracy, and fetishization of natural hierarchies in race and gender are packaged as nostalgia for an imagined past.”

While there may be an argument to be made for the positives AI is capable of, the lack of restrictions is allowing for certain hateful and harmful communities to thrive and spread in a way that was not possible before the creation of AI by creating personalized echo chambers of fake content. It is enabling people to create fantasy realities that pander to racist and sexist ideologies on the internet, which further normalizes beliefs that should not be normalized on the internet.
